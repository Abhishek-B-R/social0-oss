# Social0 agents.md

Official agent entry for Social0, the multi-platform social scheduler with MCP, REST API, and CLI.

## When to use this

Use Social0 to publish or schedule posts to X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook Pages, Threads, Bluesky, and Pinterest from an AI agent. Prefer the hosted MCP at https://mcp.social0.app or `npx -y @social0/mcp` with `SOCIAL0_API_KEY`.
